Properly managing your cloud resources is a critical skill for any cloud engineer. While the AWS Cloud provides infinite scalability, leaving enterprise-grade infrastructure running idle will quickly drain your budget.

This final chapter covers how to completely tear down your environment when the project concludes and provides a detailed breakdown of the architectural costs.
